Paleo Banana Bread

Adapted from the Paleo variation on Healthy Recipes.

Prep time: 15 minutes
Bake time: 40-50 minutes
Cooling time: About 2 hours
Makes: 12 slices

Ingredients

  • Cooking spray
  • 3 large eggs
  • 3 large ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons honey
  • 8 ounces (about 2 cups) almond flour
  • 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon (use 1-2 teaspoons for a milder flavor)
  • Pinch of kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda, or 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/3 cup chopped nuts or dark chocolate chips (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350 F. Line an 8 1/2-by-4 1/2-inch loaf pan with parchment, leaving an overhang, and lightly grease it.
  2. Whisk the eggs, mashed bananas, vanilla, and honey together.
  3. Mix in the almond flour, cinnamon, salt, and baking soda until the batter is smooth. Fold in optional nuts or chocolate.
  4. Transfer the batter to the pan and tap the pan gently on the counter to level it.
  5. Bake for 40-50 minutes, until set and a tester inserted in the center comes out clean.
  6. Use the parchment to lift the loaf onto a rack. Cool completely before slicing.
