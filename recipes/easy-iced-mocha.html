Easy Iced Mocha

Adapted from Foodie Baker.

Prep time: 15 minutes
Makes: 2 drinks

Ingredients

  • 2 shots espresso
  • 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Milk, to top up the drinks
  • Whipped cream, chocolate sauce, chocolate shavings, or ice cream (optional)

Instructions

  1. Brew the espresso. Whisk the cocoa powder and sugar together in a measuring cup.
  2. Pour in the hot espresso and whisk until the cocoa and sugar dissolve. Chill this mocha base if preparing ahead.
  3. Divide the base between two 300 ml cups. Add ice until each cup is about three-quarters full, then top with milk.
  4. Add optional toppings and serve right away.
