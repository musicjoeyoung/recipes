Garlic-Roasted Chicken Leg Quarters

Adapted from The Spruce Eats.

Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 45 minutes
Total time: 1 hour
Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 chicken leg quarters
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth, or 1/4 cup broth plus 1/4 cup dry white wine

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425 F. Lightly oil a 9-by-13-inch baking dish or line it with foil.
  2. Combine the garlic, oregano, salt, chili powder, cumin, pepper, and oil into a paste.
  3. Trim excess skin and fat from the chicken, arrange it in the dish, and rub the garlic mixture over each piece.
  4. Pour the broth (or broth and wine) into the bottom of the dish.
  5. Bake uncovered for 40-50 minutes, until the thickest part of the chicken reaches at least 165 F.
