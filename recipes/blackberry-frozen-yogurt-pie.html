Blackberry Frozen Yogurt Pie with Cracker Crust

Adapted from NYT Cooking.

Active time: About 30 minutes
Crust bake time: 20-25 minutes
Freeze time: At least 4 hours
Serves: 8-10

Ingredients

Crust

  • 6 ounces (170 g) Ritz crackers or saltines (about 50 crackers)
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 6 tablespoons (85 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled

Filling

  • 6 ounces (170 g) fresh or frozen blackberries
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 cup (240 ml) heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup (240 ml) full-fat Greek yogurt

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350 F. Finely crush the crackers with the sugar and salt. Add the melted butter and mix until evenly moistened.
  2. Press the crumbs firmly over the bottom and sides of a 9-inch pie plate. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until dry and golden; cool completely.
  3. Process the blackberries with the sugar and lemon juice until crushed.
  4. Beat the cream and condensed milk until stiff peaks form. Stir the yogurt smooth, then gently fold it into the cream mixture.
  5. Spread the filling into the cooled crust. Spoon the blackberry mixture over it and swirl together.
  6. Freeze until firm, at least 4 hours. Let stand at room temperature for about 30 minutes before slicing.
