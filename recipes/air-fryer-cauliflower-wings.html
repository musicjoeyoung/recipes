Air Fryer Cauliflower Wings

Adapted from The Foodie Eats.

Prep time: 10 minutes
Cook time: 15 minutes per batch
Serves: 6

Ingredients

  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 1/2 cup unsweetened soy milk
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 3/4 cup gluten-free 1-to-1 baking flour
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup hot sauce
  • 2 tablespoons vegan buttery spread, melted
  • 1/4 cup molasses

Instructions

  1. Whisk the flour, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and pepper together. Whisk in the soy milk and water until smooth.
  2. Dip the florets in the batter in small batches, letting excess drip off.
  3. Lightly spray the air-fryer basket. Place the florets in a single layer with space between them; cook in batches as needed.
  4. Air-fry at 350 F for 15 minutes, until the coating is dry with golden edges and the cauliflower is tender.
  5. Whisk the melted spread, hot sauce, and molasses together. Toss the cooked cauliflower in the sauce just before serving.
