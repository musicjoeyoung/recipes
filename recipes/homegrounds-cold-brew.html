Cold Brew Coffee Concentrate

A ratio-based guide adapted from Home Grounds.

Steep time: 12-24 hours
Yield: Depends on batch size

Ingredients

  • Coarsely ground coffee
  • Cold water, using 5 parts water for each 1 part coffee for a standard concentrate (or 4 parts water for a stronger concentrate)
  • Cold water or milk, for diluting
  • Ice, for serving

Instructions

  1. Combine the coffee and cold water at the chosen ratio, making sure all the grounds are wet.
  2. Cover and steep for 12-24 hours.
  3. Strain out the grounds. Refrigerate the concentrate and use within about a week.
  4. For serving, dilute standard concentrate with an equal amount of cold water or milk, then pour over ice. Adjust the dilution to taste.

Note: This source is a ratio guide rather than a fixed-yield recipe. It also suggests ready-to-drink ratios of 1 part coffee to 7 parts water for a stronger brew or 1 to 12 for a milder brew.
