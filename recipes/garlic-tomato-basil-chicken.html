Garlic Tomato Basil Chicken

Adapted from Cafe Delites.

Prep time: 5 minutes
Cook time: 15 minutes
Total time: 20 minutes
Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast fillets
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter, divided
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil, divided
  • 2 cups grape tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup shredded fresh basil
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons minced garlic (about 6 large cloves)

Instructions

  1. Pound the chicken between sheets of parchment to an even thickness. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder.
  2. Heat 2 teaspoons each of the oil and butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until golden and cooked through, about 5-6 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate and cover loosely.
  3. Add the remaining oil and butter to the skillet. Cook the garlic for about 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and cook about 2 minutes, until they begin to soften.
  4. Turn off the heat and stir in the basil. Season to taste, return the chicken to the pan, and spoon the tomato mixture over it.
