Crab Cakes

Adapted from Delish.

Prep time: 10 minutes
Total time: 30 minutes
Makes: 8 crab cakes

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon hot sauce
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1 pound jumbo lump crabmeat, picked over
  • 3/4 cup panko breadcrumbs or crushed saltines
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley
  • Canola oil, for the pan
  • 2 lemons, cut into wedges
  • Tartar sauce, for serving

Instructions

  1. Whisk the egg, mayonnaise, mustard, Worcestershire, and hot sauce together; season with salt and pepper.
  2. Gently combine the crab, breadcrumbs, and parsley, then fold in the egg mixture. Shape into 8 patties.
  3. Heat a thin layer of oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the cakes in batches, turning once, for 3-5 minutes per side, until golden and crisp.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges and tartar sauce.
