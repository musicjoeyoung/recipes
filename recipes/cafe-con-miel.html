Cafe con Miel (Spanish Coffee with Honey)

Inspired by Sugar & Soul's Cafe con Miel.

Prep time: 5 minutes
Total time: 5 minutes
Serves: 1

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 4 ounces espresso or strong brewed coffee
  • 2 ounces whole milk
  • Ground cinnamon, for topping

Instructions

  1. Add the honey to a mug or double-walled glass.
  2. Pour in the hot coffee and stir until the honey dissolves.
  3. Steam the milk to 165°F and pour it into the mug.
  4. Finish with a generous sprinkle of ground cinnamon.
