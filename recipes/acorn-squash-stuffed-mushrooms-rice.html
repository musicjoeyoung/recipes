Acorn Squash Stuffed with Mushrooms and Rice

Adapted from Martha Stewart.

Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 40 minutes
Total time: 1 hour
Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 acorn squash (about 1 pound each), halved crosswise and seeded
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1/2 pound cremini or button mushrooms, finely diced
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 3/4 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan (about 2 ounces)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 450 F. Season the squash cut sides with salt and pepper, drizzle with 1 tablespoon oil, and set cut-side down on a rimmed baking sheet. Cover tightly with foil and roast about 35 minutes, until tender.
  2. While the squash roasts, heat the remaining oil in a saucepan over medium-high. Add the mushrooms, onion, thyme, salt, and pepper; cook about 8 minutes, until the mushrooms are browned.
  3. Stir in the rice and broth and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce the heat, and cook about 20 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed.
  4. Turn on the broiler. Scoop 2-3 tablespoons of squash flesh from each half and mix it into the rice filling. Taste and season as needed.
  5. Spoon the filling into the squash halves, top with Parmesan, and broil about 2 minutes, until the cheese melts.

Note: Some filling may remain. For a vegan version, use vegetable broth and omit the Parmesan.
