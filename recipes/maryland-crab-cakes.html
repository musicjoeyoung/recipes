Maryland Crab Cakes (Little Filler)

Adapted from Sally's Baking Addiction; the provided print version links to the same recipe.

Prep time: 40 minutes, including chilling
Cook time: 15 minutes
Total time: 55 minutes
Makes: 6 large crab cakes

Ingredients

  • 1 large egg
  • 1/4 cup (60 g) mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley, or 2 teaspoons dried parsley
  • 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  • 2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Old Bay seasoning (up to 1 1/2 teaspoons for more heat)
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice, plus more for serving
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1 pound (454 g) fresh lump crab meat
  • 2/3 cup (41 g) saltine cracker crumbs (about 17-18 crackers)
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter (optional)

Instructions

  1. Whisk the egg, mayonnaise, parsley, mustard, Worcestershire, Old Bay, lemon juice, and salt. Gently fold in the crab and cracker crumbs, keeping the crab lumps as intact as possible.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or up to a day.
  3. Heat the oven to 450 F and grease a rimmed baking sheet.
  4. Portion the mixture into six 1/2-cup mounds. Compact each gently without flattening; brush with melted butter if desired.
  5. Bake for 12-14 minutes, until lightly browned. Finish with lemon juice and serve warm.
