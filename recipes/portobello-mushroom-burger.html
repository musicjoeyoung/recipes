Portobello Mushroom Burger

Adapted from Well Plated.

Prep time: 5 minutes
Marinate time: 30 minutes
Cook time: 10 minutes
Total time: 45 minutes
Makes: 2 burgers

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 portobello mushroom caps, stems and gills removed
  • 4 thin slices Havarti, provolone, or baby Swiss
  • 2 burger buns
  • Red onion, pesto, sliced tomato, and arugula, for serving

Instructions

  1. Mix the soy sauce, balsamic vinegar, olive oil, garlic, Italian seasoning, and pepper in a resealable bag. Add the mushroom caps and marinate for 30 minutes at room temperature, or up to 1 hour in the refrigerator.
  2. Heat a grill or grill pan to medium and lightly oil the grate.
  3. Grill the mushrooms for about 10 minutes total, turning and brushing with leftover marinade.
  4. During the final minute, place cheese on two caps, stack the other caps on top gill-side down, and add another slice of cheese. Lower the heat until melted.
  5. Toast the buns. Build the burgers with pesto, arugula, mushroom stacks, onion, and tomato.
