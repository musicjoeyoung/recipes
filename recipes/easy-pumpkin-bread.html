Easy Pumpkin Bread

Adapted from King Arthur Baking.

Hands-on time: 10 minutes
Bake time: 60-80 minutes
Makes: 2 loaves

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (198 g) vegetable oil
  • 2 2/3 cups (528 g) granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 scant cups (425 g) pumpkin puree, or 1 (15-ounce) can
  • 2/3 cup (152 g) water
  • 3 1/3 cups (400 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (113 g) chopped walnuts or pecans (optional)
  • 1 1/2 cups (255 g) chocolate chips (optional)
  • Coarse sparkling sugar, for the top (optional)

Optional glaze

  • 1 cup (113 g) confectioners' sugar
  • 2 tablespoons (28 g) melted butter
  • 1 tablespoon milk

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350 F. Grease two 8 1/2-by-4 1/2-inch loaf pans, or use two 9-by-5-inch pans if adding chocolate chips and nuts.
  2. Beat together the oil, sugar, eggs, pumpkin, and water.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, nutmeg, and vanilla until combined. Fold in optional nuts and chocolate chips.
  4. Divide the batter between the pans and sprinkle with sparkling sugar if using.
  5. Bake for 60-80 minutes, until a tester inserted into the center comes out clean. Cool on a rack.
  6. For the glaze, whisk the confectioners' sugar, melted butter, and milk until smooth, then drizzle over the cooled loaves.

Note: The loaves are easier to slice after resting overnight, wrapped, at room temperature.
