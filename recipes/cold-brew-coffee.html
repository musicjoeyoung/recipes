Cold Brew Coffee

Adapted from NYT Cooking.

Active time: 5 minutes
Steep time: 12 hours
Makes: 2 drinks

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup (35 g) medium-coarse ground coffee
  • 1 1/2 cups cold water for brewing
  • Additional cold water, for dilution
  • Ice
  • Milk (optional)

Instructions

  1. Stir the coffee and 1 1/2 cups cold water together in a jar. Cover and leave at room temperature for 12 hours or overnight.
  2. Strain the coffee twice through a coffee filter, fine-mesh sieve, or cheesecloth-lined sieve.
  3. Pour equal parts concentrate and water over ice, adjusting to taste. Add milk if desired.
