Mom's Pumpkin Chili with Turkey and Black Beans

Adapted from Tyrant Farms.

Prep time: 20 minutes
Cook time: 2 hours
Total time: 2 hours 20 minutes
Serves: 8

Ingredients

  • 2 large yellow onions, diced
  • 2 cups diced peppers
  • 6 garlic cloves, diced
  • 4 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 pounds ground turkey
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 (15-ounce) cans diced roasted tomatoes
  • 4 cans black beans, rinsed (can size not specified by the source)
  • 2 1/4 pounds pumpkin puree, thawed if frozen
  • 1 (6-ounce) can tomato paste
  • 3 tablespoons chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1 tablespoon sea salt, or to taste
  • 4 teaspoons oregano
  • 5 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne, or to taste
  • 2 teaspoons cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon cacao powder
  • Cheddar or pepper jack, sour cream, and sliced green onions, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onions and peppers and cook until the onions become translucent.
  2. Add the turkey and garlic. Cook, breaking up the turkey, until no longer pink.
  3. Stir in the broth, tomatoes, beans, pumpkin puree, tomato paste, and all seasonings.
  4. Bring to a boil and cook for 5 minutes, stirring so the bottom does not stick.
  5. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered for 1 1/2-2 hours, until thick. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.
